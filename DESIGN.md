# Kaya Hickin personal website

## Direction

A portrait-led editorial portfolio. The opening screen introduces Kaya as a person;
the next section shows the products. Short copy, generous space, and real imagery
carry the story. Work, biography, credentials, and contact each have a clear home.

## Visual rules

- Neutral paper and charcoal surfaces with a restrained green accent.
- Large sans-serif display type, occasional serif emphasis, comfortable body text.
- Real portraits and product assets. Never invent screenshots, activity, or results.
- Flat surfaces, fine rules, and generous spacing. No decorative dashboard grids,
  glowing cards, floating particles, or walls of equal-weight text.
- Give images prominent space and intentional crops at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Keep the face clear of text. Hero motion is subtle and disabled for reduced motion.
- Both color schemes must remain legible. Provide visible keyboard focus.

## Content and search

- No visible update timestamps, company-reported captions, or metric-definition links.
- Show credentials and awards concisely; native disclosures hold optional details.
- Keep important facts in server-rendered, accessible HTML, even without JavaScript.
- Structured data must describe the same facts visitors can access on the site.
- Preserve canonical URLs, internal links, image alt text, and the established routes.
- Keep metric provenance and accounting detail in source data and machine-readable
  records. Do not label recorded figures as live or change contributions to commits.
- Use a dated source record internally when a metric is a historical achievement.

## Assets

The portrait and San Francisco banner come from Kaya’s existing personal assets.
Dog AI imagery is from its public Apple App Store listing (app 6746574124).
MyFutureSelf screenshots and company logos use the existing website assets.
Use CSS layout to frame original assets without inventing product UI.

The MyFutureSelf image has transparent space above and below its screens. Size
it by width, and check the visible screen bounds when composing project panels.
Detailed credential structured data belongs on the credentials page alongside
the accessible disclosures, rather than repeating the full list on every page.
